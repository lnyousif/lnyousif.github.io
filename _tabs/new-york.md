  <p class="ly-city__intro">
    On March 26, 2001, I walked into the World Trade Center to start a job at a
    small insurance company, and collected my building ID like a medal. Six
    months later the building was gone, and I spent the next decade in asylum
    limbo, unable to leave the country and unable to see my family. Somewhere in
    there I became a New Yorker, and then a comedian.
  </p>

  <blockquote class="ly-pull">
    <p>
      There was a sense of invincibility in those halls. The World Trade Center
      felt like the safest, most indestructible place on earth.
    </p>
  </blockquote>
