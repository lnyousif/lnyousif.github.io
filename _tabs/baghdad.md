    <h1>Baghdad</h1>
    <p class="ly-city__years">1972&ndash;1998</p>
  </header>

  <p class="ly-city__intro">
    I was born into a family that built its own house &mdash; literally. My
    father, Noel, worked for free for years so that a contractor's idle crews
    would raise four walls for us in Al-Dora. What followed was a childhood of
    weekly family barbecues in my Uncle Samir's garden, soccer on unpaved
    streets, and two wars and a decade of sanctions running underneath all of it
    like a second soundtrack.
  </p>

  <blockquote class="ly-pull">
    <p>
      &ldquo;Shall we have a barbecue this time, or perhaps something from the
      kitchen?&rdquo; The debate over the menu was a familiar chorus, while we
      children eagerly anticipated the fun that would follow. No one ever worried
      about balancing the costs, though the joy was always shared equally.
    </p>
  </blockquote>
